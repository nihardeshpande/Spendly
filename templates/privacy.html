Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects information you provide directly, such as your email address when you register, and expense data you enter into the Service, including categories, amounts, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect automatically generated usage data such as IP addresses, browser type, and access times to help maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal and expense data solely to provide and operate the Service — to display your spending summaries, enable account login, and communicate with you about your account. Your data is not used to profile you for advertising and is not sold to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on servers operated by us or our trusted hosting providers. We retain your expense data for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, and error reporting. These service providers are only permitted to use your data as necessary to perform their functions on our behalf and are required to keep your information confidential.

5. Your Choices

You may access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings or by contacting us. You may also opt out of non-essential communications from Spendly.

6. Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how Spendly handles your data, contact us through the channels provided on the Spendly website.
